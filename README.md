# Point Quest 🌱

A cozy to-do game that turns tasks into points you spend on real rewards.

> 🖼️ *Screenshot / GIF placeholder — drop a preview here!*

## How to play

- 🌱 **Goal card** — Pick something you want and watch the number drop as you earn points!
- 🧠 **Brain Dump** — Empty your head here! Type anything you need to do and hit Enter — it becomes a bubble 🫧
- 🏷️ **Category chips** — Pick how big it is. Tiny chores = 1P, heavy tasks = 30P!
- 🎯 **Now slots** — Drag up to 3 bubbles here. That's your "right now" list!
- 🫧 **Pop** — Done? Click the bubble to pop it and grab your points!
- ⚡ **Just Did It** — Did something you didn't plan? Log it here too, it still counts!
- 🔥 **Today ring, streak & Recent** — Hit 30P a day for a bonus, keep your streak, and undo mistakes in Recent.
- 🧭 **Sidebar** — Check your month in Days 📅, your collection in Goals 🏆, change the vibe in Skin 🎨, and back up your data 💾.

## Your data

Everything is stored locally in your browser — nothing leaves your device. Moving to a new browser or device? Use **Export 💾** to save a backup file, then **Import 📂** to restore it there.
